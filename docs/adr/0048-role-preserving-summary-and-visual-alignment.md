# 0048: Preserve reading relationships through shared construction

Date: 2026-10-15

Status: Accepted

## Context

The shared activity construction retained property ownership and whole-group
adaptation, but its closed summaries discarded the roles of their values. A
model and its parameter became one string, while approval strategy and filesystem
scope became independently arranged strings. The common physical constructor
therefore could not preserve a common reading pattern. Successful allocation
could still produce a fragmented control beside an intact control.

Navigation had a related loss of meaning: an independent disclosure target's
width determined the visual column for every decorative icon. Shared alignment
proved that the rows agreed, while allowing the entire collection to consume
unnecessary leading space.

## Decision and reasons

Preserve the subject and information role of a summary until the common control
is constructed. Select the presentation of related controls at their owning
group, and preserve each chosen summary as a complete reading unit. This makes
secondary information expendable without treating the identity of the current
choice as expendable. It also prevents local wrapping from defeating an
otherwise valid group decision.

Keep visible alignment distinct from interaction extent. A decorative icon
inherits the row's action; a disclosure button has an independent action. Their
different interaction requirements must not change the shared visible origin.
The sidebar must account for its edge space as a whole, rather than accumulate
unrelated padding decisions.

Bind the resulting obligations to the production constructors. Equations that
only preserve a previous arrangement cannot establish that these reading and
interaction relationships survive projection. Actual page inspection remains
necessary to assess their visible result.
